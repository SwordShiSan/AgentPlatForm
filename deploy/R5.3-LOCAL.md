## Evidence boundaries

- Engine tests prove parsing/limits only, Desktop smoke proves conversion only, and UI fixtures prove viewer behavior only. None replaces signed Linux/runsc/DB/MQ/S3 acceptance.
- Three modern Office formats are required. Unsupported/encrypted/corrupt files fail explicitly, original downloads remain attachment responses. PDF Range requests serialize under the existing global transfer slot.
- Renderer and business sandbox share the persistent heavy slot. Common2176+heavy512+runtime128+helper64=2880MiB; caps≤2887MiB, reserve≥768MiB and root≥5GiB. No temporary uncounted Office service or runc fallback.
- Source transport closes and records its preview-specific durable release before output upload. Output is copied from bounded tmpfs before container removal, but READY/upload requires exact physical stop; callback cancellation alone is insufficient.
- Docker archive does not read tmpfs reliably. The fixed renderer uses bounded docker-java exec reads of sealed manifest/output, persists each read identity before start and confirms read-exec/callback/container termination. Office startup libraries are checked at image-build time.
- Queueing is bidirectional: an accepted environment prepare/wake remains in its existing operation/outbox while a preview owns heavy capacity; a preview also waits for an active sandbox. No valid activation fails merely because the other workload is running.
- Necessary test disk cold archives use `cold-archive-test-disk.py` with exact stopped/unmounted test root and two verified local copies before raw removal. Never production XFS or business data.

Actual runtime/engineering receipts are indexed in `evidence/r53/acceptance-index.json`; protected implementation promotion passed; final documentation/delivery identities are recorded separately. Cloud sandbox remains DEFERRED. R5.4 and R5 production release are outside this stage.
